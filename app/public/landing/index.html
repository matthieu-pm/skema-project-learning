Where Mimo stands today

Before you start

Can I book a lesson yet?

Not yet. You can try learner and teacher setup in the preview. Teacher search, bookings, payments, and lessons are still in development.

Is Mimo for learners or teachers?

Both. Learners choose their language, level, and goals. Teachers share their approach and preferences. You can choose either role in the preview.

Does AI replace the teacher?

No. We’re planning optional AI tools to help teachers draft lessons and find resources. Teachers review the content and give the final feedback.

Do I need to practice every day?

No. Mimo has no daily targets or streaks. The focus is on your goals and a pace that fits your life.
