# Mimo · Story map

Planning baseline · 16 October 2026. SKEMA workspace / SKEMA team (SKE). **8 features, 26 child user stories, 10 non-functional requirements and 13 enablers; 57 Backlog issues.**

## Product outcome
A learner practises a concrete language goal with a teacher they choose. The teacher prepares a short editable lesson and manages follow-up with less administration. These are hypotheses to validate, not demonstrated impact.

**R1 · Core tutoring:** an end-to-end chosen-teacher lesson, including manual preparation, session management, safety and private recap. Production account, booking/payment and teaching services remain to be built.

**R2 · Reduce repeated work:** reusable lessons, optional practice/progress and production earnings/payouts after commercial policy is agreed.

**R3 · Optional assistance:** external calendars and teacher-controlled AI. Core tutoring must remain usable without these.

These slices are planning proposals, not committed releases, sprint assignments, deadlines or estimates. Existing simulations are not marked Done.

## Dependencies and open decisions
- F01 learner brief + F02 teacher profile/availability enable F03 discovery.
- F03 explicit teacher choice + agreed commercial/safeguarding policies enable F04 booking.
- F01 learner goal and F04 session context inform F05 preparation; approval/share is deliberate.
- F04 session + F05 approved materials enable F06 teaching, then F07 private recap and optional practice.
- F08 and the NFRs apply throughout; production gates cannot be skipped because a frontend simulation exists.
- Decide prices/fees, cancellation/refunds/no-shows, compensation, payouts, age eligibility, guardian authority/consent, safe meetings, reporting escalation, retention/deletion and recording consent before relevant real operations. No placeholder financial rule becomes policy.

## Scope and evidence
Onboarding and the teacher Students / Schedule / Lessons / Profile workspace exist as local frontend simulations. The teacher workspace includes fictional scheduling, messages, modular lessons, AI drafts, submissions, feedback and recaps. Learner discovery/booking and all real accounts, payments, identity, video, AI, storage and synchronization are proposed. Reload resets edits; phone previews are not native binaries.

Exactly one learner and one chosen teacher per session; online or in person. Teacher retains final evaluation and publication control. Feedback stays private. Exclude automatic assignment, groups, social feeds, public leaderboards, friend-following, daily obligations, streaks and long-form courses. Public ratings/trust presentation remains unresolved. Identity checks do not establish teaching qualifications or guarantee safety. Recording/transcription is not on by default.
